STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
Module: Policy

Purpose
Standardize policy document control and organizational structure governance.

Scope
Applies to policy owners, reviewers/approvers, and structure admins.

Responsibilities
- Policy Owner: Draft/revise content.
- Reviewer/Approver: Govern approval workflow.
- Admin: Maintain org structure accuracy.
- End Users: Consume current approved versions.

Procedure
1) Draft or revise policy
2) Submit for review/approval
3) Publish approved version
4) Maintain organizational structure updates

Controls
- Version control and approval gates
- Change-history tracking
- Restricted publish/edit permissions
- Controlled distribution of approved policy
