STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
Module: Assurance

Purpose
Define audit lifecycle and SPI monitoring operations.

Scope
Applies to audit, compliance, and assurance users.

Responsibilities
- Audit Manager: Plan/schedule audits.
- Auditor: Execute audits and capture findings/evidence.
- Compliance Owner: Corrective action tracking.
- Assurance Lead: SPI performance oversight.

Procedure
1) Plan audits
2) Schedule resources/dates
3) Execute audits
4) Record findings and actions
5) Monitor closure/effectiveness
6) Review SPI dashboards/configuration

Controls
- State-based audit workflow
- Evidence/findings requirements
- Corrective action due-date tracking
- RBAC for assurance functions
