STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
Module: Listings

Purpose
Provide standardized list-based management for reports, hazards, assessments, mitigations, and investigations.

Scope
Applies to authorized users of SMS Listings pages.

Responsibilities
- Analyst/User: Search/filter/review.
- Editor/Owner: Maintain records.
- Approver: Execute approvals where authorized.
- Admin: Maintain access controls.

Procedure
1) Open target listing
2) Filter/search/sort/page
3) View/edit/approve as permitted
4) Export as needed

Controls
- Permission-based action visibility
- Validation on edit/update actions
- Consistent listing pagination/search behavior
- Audit traceability
