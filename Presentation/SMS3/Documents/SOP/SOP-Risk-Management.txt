STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
Module: Risk Management

Purpose
Define hazard reporting, report processing, technical assessment, investigations, and mitigation tracking.

Scope
Applies to authorized users in SMS Risk workflows.

Responsibilities
- Reporter: Submit complete hazard reports.
- Validator/Reviewer: Validate and route reports.
- Risk Assessor: Complete Technical Assessment Step 1-5.
- Investigator: Manage interviews/evidence and case decisions.
- Mitigation Owner/Approver: Implement and approve mitigations.

Procedure
1) Create Hazard Report
2) Process/Validate Report
3) Complete Technical Assessment (Step 1-5)
4) Execute Investigation (if required)
5) Create/Track/Approve Mitigations
6) Search and track by report/tracking ID

Controls
- RBAC on module pages/actions
- Required-field validation
- Controlled state transitions
- Audit metadata (CreatedBy/UpdatedBy/timestamps)
